Contact

Email us at contact@lambdaarchive.com. There's no contact form, no ticket number, and no autoresponder — the address goes to a person.

Corrections are the most useful thing you can send

Mods get updated, engines change, and a fix that worked last year can quietly stop working. If a guide on this site no longer matches what you're seeing, telling us is genuinely helpful — please include:

  • The page you're looking at
  • What the guide says should happen
  • What actually happened instead
  • Your game version or branch, and the mod version, if you know them

Verified corrections are applied promptly and the page's updated date is changed.

Other things worth writing about

  • Missing coverage. A mod that keeps breaking for people, or a question the existing guides don't answer.
  • You're a mod author. If we've described your work inaccurately, or you'd rather we linked somewhere else, just say — we'll change it.
  • Rights concerns. If you believe something on this site infringes your rights, contact us with the specific page and we will review it promptly.

We can't help with these: account or purchase problems with Steam, refunds, or technical support for the games themselves. Those need Steam Support. We're a reference site, not a support desk for Valve.
