Half-Life: Insecure — Black Mesa from the security desk

Mod profileHalf-Life (GoldSrc)Updated 2026

Half-Life's most durable idea isn't Gordon Freeman — it's the notion that the same catastrophe looks completely different depending on where you were standing. Valve built two expansions on that premise. Half-Life: Insecure is the fan-made version, and it picks the most thankless job in the building.

In one line: a classic-style single-player campaign for Half-Life 1 where you play Andrew Cooper, a Black Mesa security officer, through the resonance cascade and its aftermath.

What it is

Insecure is a full single-player campaign built on GoldSrc, deliberately in the style of the original game rather than trying to modernise it. You get Half-Life 1's weapons, Half-Life 1's pacing, and Half-Life 1's habit of dropping you into a corridor with more problems than ammunition.

Playing a security guard changes the texture of it. Gordon Freeman is a theoretical physicist who happens to be extraordinarily good at violence. A security officer is someone whose job was checking badges, and who is now the person everyone expects to have a plan. The mod is at its best in that gap.

It's been developed over a long period with several substantive revisions, which is unusual — most GoldSrc campaigns ship once and stop. Insecure kept getting rebalanced.

Which version to download

ModDB keeps old releases up, so the download list is longer than it needs to be. Take version 1.5, the most recent. For context on what changed:

VersionWhat it changed
1.5Current release — download this one
1.4Additions plus revisions to map design and enemy placement
1.3Fixes, and added Linux support
1.2Rebalanced the Xen chapter and cut padding

That 1.2 entry is worth reading twice. "Fixed balancing problems in the Xen chapter and reduced padding" is a mod team looking honestly at the least-loved part of Half-Life, noticing they'd reproduced its problems, and going back to cut. Most projects never do that.

Installing it

Standard GoldSrc mod, so the standard rules apply:

  1. Download the current version from the ModDB page.
  2. Extract it as a single folder inside your Half-Life directory, beside valve.
  3. Check that liblist.gam sits directly inside that folder, not one level deeper.
  4. Restart Steam so it's detected, or launch it with the -game parameter.

The full walkthrough, including what to do when it doesn't appear, is in installing GoldSrc mods correctly.

If it won't start at all, the likely cause isn't the mod. The 25th Anniversary update broke a large share of GoldSrc content — see the steam_legacy branch for the fallback, and check the mod's page for a compatibility release first.

Frequently asked

Is Insecure canon?

No. It's a fan work and sits outside the official storyline. Blue Shift and Opposing Force are the canonical alternate-perspective stories.

Does it run on Linux or Steam Deck?

Linux support was added in version 1.3. As with any GoldSrc mod, the engine-version issue applies the same way it does on Windows.

How long is it?

A full campaign rather than a short level pack, and it's been actively trimmed — version 1.2 specifically cut padding. Expect a substantial single-player sitting rather than an evening's novelty.

Can I play it alongside other mods?

Installed alongside, yes — each mod is its own folder. Combined into one, no, if both ship compiled libraries. See the data-vs-code rule.
