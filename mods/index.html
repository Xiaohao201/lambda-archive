Half-Life mods

Section indexGoldSrc & SourceUpdated 2026

Half-Life has one of the longest-running mod scenes in PC gaming, and also one of the most confusing to get started with — because "Half-Life mods" means two incompatible things depending on which game you own. Sort that out first and most install problems disappear before they happen.

The one distinction that matters

Half-Life 1 and Half-Life 2 do not share a mod format. They do not share an engine, a file structure, or an install location.

A GoldSrc mod dropped into sourcemods will never appear. A Source mod placed inside the Half-Life folder will never appear either. This single mismatch is behind a large share of "the mod didn't install" posts.

Why so many mods broke recently

Half-Life's 25th Anniversary update rebuilt significant parts of the GoldSrc engine. It was good for the base game and bad for compatibility: a large share of existing mods, and the official expansion packs, stopped working correctly on the new build.

Two routes out, in order of preference:

  1. Check whether the mod shipped a compatibility patch. Many of the actively maintained ones did. This is better than the alternative because it leaves your main installation on the current build.
  2. Switch to the legacy engine. Valve archived the pre-update build as a public beta branch named steam_legacy, selectable from the game's Properties › Betas menu.

Frequently asked

Can I install a Half-Life 1 mod into Half-Life 2?

No. Different engines, different file formats, different install locations. There is no conversion — a GoldSrc mod only runs on GoldSrc.

Do mods disable Steam achievements?

Mods run as separate entries and generally don't award the base game's achievements. This is a side effect of how mods are launched, not a penalty.

Are Half-Life mods safe to install?

Mods from established authors on ModDB are as safe as any downloaded software, but they ship unsigned .dll files that antivirus tools sometimes quarantine. Download from the author's official page rather than a re-upload, and be deliberate about any exclusion you add.

Will installing a mod break my normal game?

A correctly installed mod lives in its own folder and never touches valve. The exception is a launch option left in place — -game will keep starting the mod until you clear it.
