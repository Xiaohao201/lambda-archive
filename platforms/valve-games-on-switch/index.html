Valve games on Switch — why Portal made it and Half-Life didn't

Platform guideNintendo SwitchUpdated 2026

The short version, because it's the thing people are actually searching for:

Half-Life is not on Nintendo Switch. No official release exists, for any game in the series. What is on Switch is Portal and Portal 2 — which are set in the same universe, which is exactly why the question keeps coming up.

Why the absence isn't technical

The usual assumption is that Switch hardware couldn't handle Half-Life 2, or that Source wouldn't run on it. Neither holds up, and there's a specific reason to be confident about that.

NVIDIA Lightspeed Studios — the studio Valve used for the Portal collection — had already ported Half-Life 2 to the Android-based NVIDIA Shield.

Read that again in the right order:

  • A studio ported Half-Life 2 to a mobile-class NVIDIA device.
  • Valve later hired that same studio to bring Portal to a mobile-class NVIDIA device.
  • They shipped Portal, not Half-Life.

So the engineering was already proven, by the same team, on comparable silicon. Whatever kept Half-Life off the Switch, "it wouldn't run" isn't it. Valve has never publicly given a reason, and this page isn't going to invent one — but the technical explanation can be set aside.

Valve has its own handheld

The context worth adding: Valve sells the Steam Deck, and Half-Life runs on it natively.

That last word matters. Valve ships official Linux builds of Half-Life and Half-Life 2, and the Steam Deck runs Linux — so these aren't games limping through a compatibility layer, they're running on a platform they were properly built for. Steam Deck support arrived as part of the anniversary updates.

A company with its own handheld, its own store, and native builds already running on it has considerably less reason to put its flagship series on a competitor's device. That's an observation about incentives, not an insider explanation.

What about "Half-Life on Switch" listings you've seen?

Searches for this turn up a few recurring things, none of which are what they look like:

  • Homebrew and unofficial ports. Community projects, not Valve releases, requiring a modified console.
  • Portal listings. Sometimes described as "Valve's Half-Life universe games", which is technically true and practically misleading.
  • Fan concept art and mock-ups. Convincing box art for a product that doesn't exist.
  • Similar-looking shooters. Retro-styled FPS games on the eShop that get recommended to people asking this question.

If a listing claims an official Half-Life release for Switch, it's wrong.

Frequently asked

Will Half-Life ever come to Switch?

Unknowable, and nothing currently points that way. Valve's handheld strategy runs through its own hardware.

Is Portal on Switch a good version?

It's the complete Portal and Portal 2 experience on a handheld, which is the main thing. If you specifically want Portal portable, it's the only official route.

Is Half-Life: Alyx on Switch?

No. It's a VR-exclusive PC title and requires VR hardware the Switch doesn't have.

Can I play Half-Life on Steam Deck?
