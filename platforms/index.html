Where you can play Half-Life

Section indexPlatformsUpdated 2026

Half-Life is nearly thirty years old and runs on more things than most games released this year. It also has a long tail of ports and platforms that either died, never shipped, or exist only as rumour — so here's the honest version.

The unusual advantage: Valve ships official Linux builds of both Half-Life and Half-Life 2. That single fact is why they run so well on Steam Deck, Linux desktops and Chromebooks, while newer games need translation layers to limp along.

The Switch question

It comes up constantly, so: there is no official Half-Life release for Nintendo Switch. Valve publishes on its own storefront and its own hardware, and the handheld it wants you to play Half-Life on is the Steam Deck, which runs it natively.

Anything presenting itself as Half-Life on Switch is either an unofficial port or a different game entirely. Treat it accordingly.

What "native Linux" actually buys you

It's worth being precise, because it changes what works:

  • The games themselves run properly. No compatibility layer, no translation overhead, no per-title tinkering.
  • Data-only mods generally work. Maps, models, sounds and sprites are platform-independent.
  • Code mods generally don't. A mod shipping compiled Windows libraries is still shipping Windows binaries. That rules out most gameplay overhauls — the data-vs-code distinction decides it.

Frequently asked

Does Half-Life run on a Mac?

Support for older Valve titles on macOS has shifted over the years as Apple dropped 32-bit software. Check the store page's platform icons for the current answer rather than trusting any guide, including this one.

Will my saves transfer between platforms?

Steam Cloud handles saves where a game supports it. Between entirely different engines — a phone reimplementation and desktop Steam, say — assume they won't.

What's the best way to play on a handheld?

Steam Deck, by a distance. Native Linux builds plus official support means it works without configuration, which is not true of most games its age.

Something here is out of date.

Platform support changes and this page is maintained by hand — if you're looking at something that contradicts it, tell us and we'll correct it.
